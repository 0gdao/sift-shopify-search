# Product brief

## Narrowest wedge

**Job:** “Show me credible products from independent Shopify merchants, let me compare the real offers, then get me out of the way.”

The first release is a no-account, text-first comparison search. It deliberately avoids wish-list accounts, payments, merchant ingestion, ads, and a proprietary index. Those features create a platform before demand exists.

## User promise

- Search eligible products across Shopify, not one storefront at a time.
- See current price ranges, availability signals, seller count, ratings, and options.
- Compare up to three products without losing the result set.
- Inspect merchant offers and continue to the merchant's checkout.

## Product principles

1. **Trust over lock-in.** Merchant identity and handoff are always explicit.
2. **Zero setup.** The first search requires no account or onboarding.
3. **Live over exhaustive.** Never imply that an estimated count or eligible catalog is every product on the internet.
4. **Relevance integrity.** Preserve Shopify's result order; do not silently sell ranking.
5. **Fast recovery.** Empty, partial, rate-limited, and configuration states tell the user what to do next.

## Success measures

- Search-to-product-detail rate
- Product-detail-to-merchant click rate
- Comparison usage and resulting merchant click rate
- Search error and empty-result rate
- P50/P95 search latency

## Deliberately deferred

- Image and multimodal search
- Buyer-linked personalization
- Promoted placements and affiliate payout flows
- Accounts, saved searches, and alerts
- Native checkout and order tracking
