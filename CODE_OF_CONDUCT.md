# Code of Conduct

Sift contributors are expected to collaborate respectfully and constructively.

- Be welcoming, patient, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing sensitive information.
- Accept feedback in good faith and focus on the project's users.
- Report unacceptable behavior privately through [ECORN](https://ecorn.agency/).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive community.
